Privacy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Games@Work is run by %OPERATOR%, who is responsible for your data. Questions or requests: %CONTACT%.

What we keep

  • From your Google sign-in: your Google account ID, your name and the address of your profile picture. If Google gives no name, we store your email address instead.
  • Your games: for each puzzle you open, when you started and finished, whether you won, your score, the hints you used and, for football games, your guesses.
  • Your network: the people you connected with through an invite link.
  • Request logs: our host keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser, time) for up to 30 days to keep the service running and secure.

Your browser also keeps your unfinished moves on your device, so a reload doesn’t lose them.

Why

Only to run the game you signed up for: daily puzzles, your streaks and statistics, and leaderboards with your connections. There are no ads, no analytics or tracking cookies, and we never sell or share your data.

Who can see it

Your connections see your name, picture and today’s results. Anyone you send your invite link to sees your name. “Faster than X% of players” is worked out from everyone’s times without showing names.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in for 30 days. Signing in loads Google’s sign-in button, which is covered by Google’s privacy policy.

Where and for how long

Data is stored on Google Cloud in the European Union (Belgium) until you delete your account. Delete account under your profile removes your profile, every game and your connections straight away; logs expire within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or object to how it is used, by writing to %CONTACT%. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Portugal, the CNPD). Games@Work is not meant for children under 16.

Changes

If this page changes, the date above changes too.
